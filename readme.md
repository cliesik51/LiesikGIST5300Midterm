These are 4 popular regional fast food burger restaurants that are present in different areas in the United States. In-N-Out started in California but have spread across the American West and is known worldwide. Shake Shack started as a burger stand in New York and has quickly spread across the majority of the United States. Checkers and Rally's started as 2 separate restaurants in the American South; after merging into one restaurant, they have spread from coast to coast in states along the southern border. Cookout started in North Carolina and spread across the majority of the American South. My brother lives in Savannah, GA, and swears this is better than In-N-Out by a mile.

It is constantly argued about which brand has the best food, but it would also be interesting to see how these locations compare to each other on a spatial scale.

#### Analysis

Looking at the distribution of the markers, we can see that each chain has its own strongholds located where they originated from. That isn't surprising to see, but it is interesting that every chain but Cookout has locations in Arizona, California, Nevada, and Texas. Cookout has yet to venture west of the Mississippi River. Additionally, if it weren't for Texas and the I-25 corridor from Colorado Springs to Fort Collins, there would be a gap along the Great Plains and the Front Range of the Rockies where none of the chains had locations. As these businesses expand outside of their initial footprint, they appear to focus primarily on larger urban areas. Especially Shake Shack, with the East Coast brand holding locations in cities like Kansas City, Denver, Salt Lake City, Portland, and Seattle. Obviously, this map won't be the deciding factor in which brand has the best food, but it definitely highlights the reach that some of these once "regional" chains have acquired.
